Stone prices (rate per cube)

Set the default price for 1 cube of each stone type. When entering cubes on Daily sales, total prices will be calculated automatically.

Automatic calculation

Once configured below, typing any cube quantity (e.g. 2.5 cubes) on the Daily sales page will instantly multiply by your set rate and fill the price. You can still edit any calculated price directly in the sales table for special discounts or custom agreements.
